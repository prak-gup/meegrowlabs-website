Concept Paper · Table 2

Three principles

Non-invasive

The card stays. No new URC hardware, no change to the beneficiary-facing procedure. Verification is a layer added on top of the card-presentation event, not a replacement for it.

Data-sovereign

Hosted on NIC MeghRaj / MeitY-approved Government Cloud in a pilot — nothing on a commercial cloud. This demonstrator runs fully offline, in this browser, so the same discipline is visible right now.

Cost-proportionate

Software-led architecture, no proprietary vendor lock-in, open-source AI stack where possible. Capex scales with what is actually needed at 4,000 URCs, not with what is impressive.

What this demonstrator is NOT
  • Not connected to any CSD, SPARSH, PCDA, AFD or UIDAI system — every request stays inside this browser.
  • All beneficiaries, URCs, cards, complaints and vendors are fictional, seeded data.
  • Entitlement ceilings, prices and volumes are illustrative only — they are not CS Dte policy values.
  • "AI" here is shown as transparent, inspectable rules, so every output can be traced to its evidence. Trained models can replace these rules in a pilot; the contract that every output carries its evidence stays.
